Instructions – Please read carefully
  1. The exam runs in full-screen mode. Do not exit full screen.
  2. Do not switch tabs, open another window or any other app during the exam.
  3. Copy, paste, right-click, screenshots and printing are disabled.
  4. Every such attempt is recorded as a violation. After the limit, your exam is submitted automatically.
  5. Each question has four options. Select one and click Save & Next.
  6. Use Mark for Review for questions you want to revisit. Use the question palette to jump to any question.
  7. When the timer reaches zero, your exam is submitted automatically.
  8. Your result is shown immediately after submission and is recorded by the school.
